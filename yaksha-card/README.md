# Yaksha Rituals - Business Card & Google QR Studio

This folder contains the complete, editable business card design and interactive studio tailored for **Yaksha Rituals**, featuring the brand's authentic circular gold emblem logo.

---

## 🔤 Brand Design & Typography

- **Brand Emblem Logo**:
  - **Front Card Watermark**: The official circular gold Yaksha Rituals logo emblem (`YR` seal with botanical flourishes) is now embedded as the central watermark in place of `य`.
  - **Back Card Top Tab**: The white accent pill tab now displays the Yaksha Rituals circular gold emblem in place of `य`.
  - **Back Card Brand Seal**: Prominently features the circular gold logo above `YAKSHA RITUALS`.
- **Headings & Main Titles**: **Marcellus** (Classic refined serif)
  - Applied to `YAKSHA`, card titles, and the `JASMINE20` coupon code.
- **Body & Paragraphs**: **Jost** (Clean geometric sans-serif)
  - Applied to `RITUALS`, brand narrative descriptions, contact numbers, email, and labels.
- **Clean 3-Row Contact Box (No Founder / Name)**:
  - **Row 1**: Highlighted coupon badge: **`COUPON: JASMINE20`**
  - **Row 2**: Phone: **`+91 98765 43210`**
  - **Row 3**: Email: **`connect@yaksharitual.com`**
- **Google Business Profile QR**:
  - Contains the authentic Google Business QR code with the multi-colored border and central `G` emblem, ending with `yaksharitual.com`.

---

## 🗂 Files Included

1. **`index.html`** - The full **Interactive Card Studio & Editor**.
   - Open in your browser: [http://localhost:4242/](http://localhost:4242/) or directly via file.
   - Live side-by-side editing of Front & Back cards.
   - Built-in **Export to 300 DPI PNG**, **Export to Vector SVG**, and **Print / PDF** commands.

2. **`yaksha-card-front.svg`** - Standalone Vector SVG file for the Front side.
   - Embedded with the Yaksha Rituals circular logo watermark, Marcellus & Jost typography, and the clean 3-row box (`JASMINE20`, Phone, Email).

3. **`yaksha-card-back.svg`** - Standalone Vector SVG file for the Back side.
   - Embedded with the Yaksha Rituals logo in the top accent pill, brand seal, authentic Google Business Profile QR, and `yaksharitual.com`.

4. **`yaksha-logo.png`** - The official Yaksha Rituals circular gold emblem logo.
5. **`google-qr.png`** - The official Google Business review QR image.
